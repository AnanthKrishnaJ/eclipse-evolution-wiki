How Growth Works

Powers and hidden skills both have five levels, but they grow in different ways.

Divine powers

Use it and it evolves

  • Active powers gain 1 point per cast, and 2 more when the cast lands a killing blow.
  • Passive powers gain 1 point for every enemy you defeat.
  • The power Skill Evolution doubles every point, for all your powers.
Points to next level = 6 × level²
Active power = 1 + 0.15 × (Lv − 1), then × 1.25 when evolved
Passive effect = 1 + 0.15 × (Lv − 1), + 0.25 when evolved
Cooldown = base × (1 − 0.05 × (Lv − 1))

On/off effects such as Appraisal or Treasure Sense work fully from level 1 and do not scale.

Hidden skills

Repeat it and you adapt

Each hidden skill watches one counter, such as slimes defeated or seconds spent in the rain. When the counter passes a threshold, the skill gains a level. Several skills can watch the same counter, so one habit can teach you more than one thing.

Most thresholds = base × 1, 3, 7, 15, 30
Effect at level L = per-level effect × L

Hidden skill bonuses add on top of your powers. Twenty-five secret skills keep their names hidden in the game until you make progress on them.

At the altar

How the choice works

  • Carry up to 3 chosen gifts, paid from 7 soul points.
  • Only one Legendary or Divine gift fits in a soul.
  • Spend every soul point and Fate's Gacha drops one bonus gift from the powers you did not choose.
  • The four hidden powers are free and earned in the Kochi opening.
  • Active powers fill your 4 power slots (Z, X, C, V) in the order you get them.

Class Skills

Class skills do not evolve. Each character level gives one skill point to spend in the talent tree, up to rank 3 per skill.
